# Future of War Timeline

An interactive, scrolling timeline from the theory of why war happens (1995), through today's overlooked asymmetries (2026), to 2036, 2046 and the world after AGI. Eleven simulations you can run in the browser.

Live: https://hillaryyang.github.io/future-of-war-timeline/

Single self-contained `index.html`: no build step, no dependencies. Content lives in the data block at the top of the script; simulations and layout follow.

Stations dated 2026 or earlier are sourced facts or labeled analysis. Everything from 2036 on is a labeled projection. The simulations are simplified illustrative models, not military forecasts.
